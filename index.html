ABOUT US

We connect travellers with authentic Sri Lankan cultural experiences, guided by local communities who share their traditions, crafts, cuisine and stories.
From village kitchens to traditional mask making, every experience is led by local hosts — and a fair share of what you pay stays with them.

To support local communities by sharing their culture, traditions and daily life with travellers, while creating meaningful and sustainable tourism experiences.
